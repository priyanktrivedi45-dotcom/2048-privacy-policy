2048 Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-09-23 · Android package: com.colormerge.game2048

About this policy

The Data Sequence provides the 2048 Android game. This policy explains information handled by the game and its advertising services. The game does not require an account or ask you to submit a name, email address, payment details, contacts, photographs, microphone recordings or precise GPS location.

Game data stored on your device

The game stores settings and progress on your device, including board preferences, best scores, audio preferences, levels, experience, coins, achievements, gameplay totals, streaks and daily challenge or reward dates. These values support gameplay and persistence between sessions. The game does not include a developer-operated account, cloud leaderboard or server that receives these saved game values. Android device backup or transfer may retain or restore app data according to your device settings.

Advertising and service providers

The game integrates Google AdMob for banner and interstitial advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform for privacy choices. Depending on device settings, consent and the advertising service, Google may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, advertising or other device identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostic information. These services use data to deliver and measure advertising, operate and improve the service, and detect fraud. Data may be handled by Google and advertising partners according to your choices and their applicable policies. The game does not include a separate Firebase Analytics integration.

Your advertising choices

Where required and configured, a privacy message lets you make advertising choices. The app checks Google's consent eligibility before requesting ads. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Settings displays Ad privacy choices so you can revisit those choices. Your Android settings may also let you reset or delete your advertising ID. Limiting personalization does not necessarily remove all ads or all data processing needed for basic ad delivery and fraud prevention.

Retention and deletion

Game preferences and progress remain in app storage until removed or overwritten. You can remove local game data through Android Settings, Apps, 2048, Storage, Clear storage; this erases saved progress and preferences. Uninstalling also removes the app's local data, although device backups may persist under your backup settings. The Data Sequence has no game-account database to delete. Google and its partners control retention of data processed by their services; consult their privacy information and available controls for those records.

Security and international processing

Android protects app storage using its platform security mechanisms. Google's advertising services use encrypted network transport. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Third-party services may process information on servers outside your country under their applicable privacy terms and safeguards.

Age suitability

The game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. The game does not ask you to enter your birth date. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game or a support request, contact The Data Sequence using the address below. Age-related privacy protections and consent requirements can vary by country.

Contact and privacy requests

Use the privacy contact listed below for questions or requests about information handled by The Data Sequence. Do not include passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary sensitive information. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include in that communication.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The updated date identifies this version. The app includes an offline copy; later installed versions may contain an updated copy. Once published, the public policy address below provides the online version.
